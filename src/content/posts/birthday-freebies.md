For a while now, I have been curious to see how far I can stretch birthday discounts. Until recently, I had never done the research. I had Claude spawn a bunch of subagents to scan different sectors like food, retail, etc. to see what birthday offers they provided. The results are less promising than I had anticipated, but there were still plenty of places offering some kind of discount or free item.

## The findings

Here's what a few hundred rewards terms pages taught me:

- 🎁 **Small stuff, not free items**: most places give a discount or a free
  add-on (guac at Chipotle, queso at Torchy's, a sweet at Alamo Drafthouse),
  and a fully free entree is rare.
- 🪦 **The good ones are dying**: Red Robin's burger, Chipotle's entree, Alamo's
  free ticket, Dunkin's drink, and AMC's free popcorn are gone, Raising Cane's
  is down to a drink, and Sprinkles and JOANN closed outright. Kerbey Lane and
  JuiceLand show up in every roundup but have no birthday reward at all.
- 📅 **Join early**: most programs want your birthday on file well ahead, like
  7 days for Starbucks and Benihana, 14 for Jimmy John's, and 7 business days
  before the month for J.Crew.
- 🧾 **Buy something first**: a lot of free entrees only go to members with a
  recent purchase, like Jersey Mike's (a sub in the prior 12 months), Jimmy
  John's (anything in the prior 6), and Starbucks (one purchase that year).
- ⏳ **Read the window**: a birthday reward can last the day itself (Starbucks
  Green), a week (Starbucks Gold, Pluckers), 30 days (Dutch Bros, Chick-fil-A),
  or the whole month (Uptown Cheapskate, Academy).
- 🏆 **Tiers gate the good stuff**: Potbelly gives new members a cookie and its
  top tier an entree, and Smoothie King charges $2 for the smoothie unless
  you're top tier. CAVA works the same way.

## The verdict

One of the busiest mornings I have had in a long time and I made it to volleyball only 5 minutes late. 

All in, I saved $47.29 from birthday rewards:

| Where | What I got | Saved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smoothie King | 20 oz The Hulk Cold Brew Coffee ($7.69, paid $2) | $5.69 |
| Starbucks | Impossible Breakfast Sandwich | $6.25 |
| Dutch Bros | Large protein vanilla latte | $6.45 |
| Torchy's | Queso | $9.95 |
| Uptown Cheapskate | 15% off my purchase | $16.00 |
| Chipotle | Guac | $2.95 |
| **Total** | | **$48.62** |

Armed with this knowledge, I know that I need to start prepping for birthday rewards earlier next year: create rewards accounts and make at least one purchase at these plces within a month of my birthday. I am will be sure to target the free sandwich from Jimmy John's and $15 off at Academy for next year.
