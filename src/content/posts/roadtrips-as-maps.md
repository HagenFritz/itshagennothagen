I wanted somewhere to document road trips and have them sitting somewhere I
could come back to. I was also hoping that reframing these drives from "let's
get to our destination" to "let's go on a journey" would make them more
enjoyable:

> To finish the moment, to find the journey's end in every step of the road, to
> live the greatest number of good hours, is wisdom.
>
> <cite>Ralph Waldo Emerson, "Experience" (1844)</cite>

In this case, it would be both since the destination is always a cool spot that
I am going on vacation for, even if it is a work trip.

## The Map

Instantly I knew that I wanted a map with the route drawn on it with points of
interest pinned. No text on the page until you click a spot to learn more about
it. Any photos I took would appear too. Thus, the
[/albums/roadtrips](/albums/roadtrips) album was born.

- 🗺️ **Route**: The route follows real roads, using my Google Maps timeline
  data to copy the route onto [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/).
  [Timeline data](https://support.google.com/maps/answer/6258979) is pretty neat
  (if you don't mind being tracked even more), it provides an exact breadcrumb
  trail of your location over time.
- 📍 **Stops**: Every stop is a pin, and tapping one opens a card next to it
  with a photo (or more) and a couple of sentences. The pin provides more
  context: what is nearby, the geography, and where it sits along the route.
  Each photo snapped with my iPhone has GPS coordinates attached to it so I
  can accurately pin the photos directly to the location with ease.
  Especially useful if I decide to take a random photo from the road of a
  Wind Turbine.

## Two Trips In

Two so far, Austin to Cinnamon Shore and back. The
[drive down](/albums/roadtrips/cinnamon-shore) went inland through small towns.
The
[drive back](/albums/roadtrips/cinnamon-shore-return) took the ferry and the
causeway.

More to come and I am excited to add more trips! I am starting to think less of
a drive to a new place as something I have to do, and more of an exciting way to
see and experience new things.
